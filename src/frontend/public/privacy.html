Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

1. 資料收集

我們收集您提供的基本資料，包括透過 Google、Facebook 或 Line 登入時取得的姓名、電子郵件位址及大頭貼。此外，我們也會收集您主動上傳至區塊鏈的文字或檔案資料。

1. Data Collection

We collect basic information you provide, including your name, email address, and profile picture obtained through Google, Facebook, or Line login. Additionally, we collect the text or file data you voluntarily upload to the blockchain.

2. 資料使用

您的資料主要用於提供服務、辨識使用者身份、管理您的「刺青」紀錄以及點數系統。我們不會將您的個人識別資料出售給第三方。

2. Use of Data

Your data is primarily used to provide our services, identify users, manage your "tattoo" records, and maintain the points system. We do not sell your personally identifiable information to third parties.

3. 區塊鏈特性（重要）

請注意，一旦您選擇將資料「刺進」區塊鏈，該資料將會永久存在於公共區塊鏈網路上，且無法被刪除或修改。本服務無法控制已寫入區塊鏈的資料。

3. Blockchain Nature (Important)

Please note that once you choose to "tattoo" data onto the blockchain, that data will exist permanently on the public blockchain network and cannot be deleted or modified. This service has no control over data already written to the blockchain.

4. 第三方整合

我們使用第三方登入服務（Google, Facebook, Line）。這些服務可能會根據其自身的隱私權政策收集您的資料。建議您查閱這些平台的隱私條款。

4. Third-Party Integration

We use third-party login services (Google, Facebook, Line). These services may collect your data according to their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the privacy terms of these platforms.

5. 資料安全

我們採取適當的安全措施來保護您的資料免於遭受未經授權的存取。然而，網路傳輸並非百分之百安全，我們無法保證絕對的安全性。

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
