User Data Deletion / 刪除使用者資料

如何申請刪除資料？

如果您希望從我們的系統中刪除您的個人資訊（如姓名、電子郵件等），請發送電子郵件至：

saltycatchen@gmail.com

我們將在接獲申請後的 14 個工作天 內處理完畢。

How to Request Data Deletion?

If you wish to delete your personal information (such as name, email, etc.) from our system, please send an email to:

saltycatchen@gmail.com

We will complete the process within 14 working days after receiving the request.

區塊鏈上的資料（重要說明）

請注意，由於區塊鏈的不可篡改性，任何已成功「刺入」區塊鏈的文字或檔案絕對無法被刪除、修改或移除。我們的資料刪除僅限於存儲在我們伺服器上的個人帳號資訊。

Data on Blockchain (Important Note)

Please note that due to the immutable nature of blockchain, any text or file successfully "tattooed" onto the blockchain CANNOT be deleted, modified, or removed. Our data deletion is limited to the personal account information stored on our servers.

第三方登入權限

除了寄送郵件外，您也可以直接在您的 Google, Facebook 或 Line 帳號設定中，移除對 Digital Tattoo 的授權。

Third-Party Login Permissions

In addition to sending an email, you can also directly remove Digital Tattoo's authorization in your Google, Facebook, or Line account settings.
